# Shemai

## Ingredients
- 200 g vermicelli
- 1 litre milk
- Sugar to taste
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- Cardamom
- Chopped nuts or raisins (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat the ghee in a pan.
2. Lightly fry the vermicelli until golden.
3. Heat the milk in a separate pot.
4. Add the vermicelli and cook until soft.
5. Add sugar and cardamom.
6. Stir until the mixture reaches the desired consistency.
7. Add nuts or raisins and serve warm or chilled.
