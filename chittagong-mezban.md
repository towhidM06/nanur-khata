# Chittagong Mezban

## Ingredients
- Beef
- Onion
- Ginger and garlic paste
- Red chilli powder
- Turmeric
- Coriander and cumin powder
- Salt
- Oil
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Wash and prepare the beef.
2. Heat oil and cook the onions until soft.
3. Add ginger, garlic and the spices.
4. Add the beef and mix well.
5. Cook until the meat begins to soften.
6. Add water and simmer until the beef is tender.
7. Adjust the seasoning and serve with rice.

## Serving Note
Mezban beef is traditionally served with rice and is prepared for family gatherings.
