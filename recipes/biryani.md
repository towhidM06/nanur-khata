# Eid Biryani

## Ingredients
- 1 kg basmati rice
- 1 kg chicken
- 2 onions, sliced
- Ginger and garlic paste
- Biryani spices
- Plain yogurt
- Oil or ghee
- Salt to taste
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Wash and soak the rice.
2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger, garlic and spices.
3. Fry the onions until golden.
4. Cook the chicken until nearly tender.
5. Boil the rice separately until partly cooked.
6. Layer the rice and chicken in a pot.
7. Add fried onions and a little ghee.
8. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice and chicken are fully cooked.
9. Serve hot with salad or raita.
Nanu.s biryani uses cardamom, cinnamon and bay leaves.

## Spice Correction
Add cardamom along with the other biryani spices.
