# Halua

## Ingredients
- Semolina
- Sugar
- Ghee
- Water

## Preparation
Mix the ingredients and cook slowly until the mixture thickens.

## Remaining Work
Add the quantities and complete the instructions.

## Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan.
2. Add semolina and roast gently.
3. Prepare sugar syrup with water.
4. Carefully add the syrup to the roasted semolina.
5. Stir until the mixture thickens.
6. Serve warm.
