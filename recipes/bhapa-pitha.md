# Bhapa Pitha

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1.5 cups date palm jaggery (gur)
- 1 cup grated coconut
- A pinch of salt
- Warm water as needed

## Instructions
1. Mix the rice flour with a little salt.
2. Sprinkle warm water over the flour until it becomes slightly moist and crumbly.
3. Place a layer of rice flour in a pitha mould.
4. Add date palm jaggery and grated coconut in the middle.
5. Cover with another layer of rice flour.
6. Steam the pitha until fully cooked.
7. Serve warm during winter.
